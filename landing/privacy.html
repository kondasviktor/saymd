What saymd is

The saymd CLI runs on your computer. Speech audio is sent to the speech-to-text provider you chose — Google Gemini, OpenAI, Deepgram, or ElevenLabs — using your own API key (BYOK). We do not host speech-to-text or sell transcription credits. The saymd.app website handles Pro subscriptions and license delivery.

CLI — what we do not collect

  • No telemetry or analytics in the CLI.
  • We do not receive your audio, transcripts, or prompt files from the CLI.
  • Your API keys are stored locally in ~/.saymd/config.json on your machine.
  • Temporary local audio is deleted after a successful Markdown write. On API failure, the CLI keeps the file and prints a retry path.

Each provider's handling of audio you send with your own key is governed by that provider's terms and privacy policy.

Website and purchases

When you buy saymd Pro, we process data needed to complete the transaction:

  • Name, email, billing address, tax ID (if provided) — via Stripe Checkout.
  • Payment status, subscription plan, Stripe customer and session IDs.
  • License key issued after successful payment (shown on the success page; stored in our database).

Full card details are handled by Stripe and are not stored by saymd. After purchase you can manage card, invoices, and cancellation in the Stripe Customer Portal (Manage subscription) — there is no saymd account or login.

Service providers

  • Stripe — payments, subscriptions, receipts.
  • Neon — database for customers, licenses, and subscription status.
  • Vercel — website hosting and serverless API routes.
  • Google (Gemini) — when you use Gemini as your STT or structuring provider (your key).
  • OpenAI — when you use OpenAI as your STT or structuring provider (your key).
  • Deepgram — when you use Deepgram as your STT provider (your key).
  • ElevenLabs — when you use ElevenLabs as your STT provider (your key).

The website does not use advertising cookies or a marketing email product. Essential hosting logs may be kept for security and debugging.
